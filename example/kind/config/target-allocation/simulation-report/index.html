Method & source

Each run: 360 cycles × 5 seconds = 30 simulated minutes; major changes every 30 cycles. Baseline: 1,100–2,000 targets with churn, 1,500 otherwise; six instances, or 3–10 with membership changes. Size mix: 75% at 50–500 series, 20% at 1k–5k, 4.5% at 10k–30k, 0.5% at 100k–400k. Size scenarios include growth/shrink and ±5% noise. Sticky uses K=6 windows and six-cycle sustained overload before repairs. Load uses current raw sizes, including idle instances. Sensitivity changes one parameter at a time with fixed deterministic identities: not random-seed trials or production traces. Sizes are supplied immediately; async probing is not modeled. Worst balance and worst movement can occur in different cycles. Source: recorded simulation results, “Sticky placement experiment” and “Parameter sensitivity”; operator simulation_test.go and simulation_sticky_test.go.
